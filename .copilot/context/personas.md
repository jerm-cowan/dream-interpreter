# User Personas — Dream Reflection

Two personas, representing the two most common motivations for opening this app. Both should be able to move through the full entry → constellation → reveal → synthesis journey, filtering lenses and adjusting tone as they like.

---

## Persona 1: "Maya, the Morning Processor"

**Age/context:** 29, marketing coordinator, phone always within reach.

**Scenario:** Maya wakes up from a vivid, slightly unsettling dream — she was back in her childhood home, but the rooms kept rearranging. She can't shake the feeling of it and reaches for her phone before she's even out of bed.

**Goals:**
- Make sense of a feeling, not just a plot.
- Get something in under a couple of minutes.
- Feel like the app "gets" that this was emotionally significant, without being melodramatic about it.

**How she uses the journey:** She types her dream on the simple entry screen, keeps the tone control on Reflective, and watches the constellation reveal with genuine curiosity. She selects just the Psychology lens first — she wants the emotional read before anything else — then goes back and adds Neuroscience out of curiosity once she's sat with the first answer. The Reflection Questions at the end are what she screenshots to think about later.

---

## Persona 2: "Theo, the Curious Skeptic"

**Age/context:** 34, software engineer, enjoys pop psychology and folklore but doesn't believe dreams "mean" anything literal.

**Scenario:** Theo's partner mentioned a bizarre dream at breakfast (being chased by a talking vending machine) and joked "what do you think that means?" Theo pulls out Dream Reflection half as a joke, half genuinely curious.

**Goals:**
- Compare lenses for fun and conversation.
- See where different schools of thought actually *agree*.
- Not be talked down to or told the dream "means" something definite.

**How he uses the journey:** He flips the tone control to Playful right away and is delighted the regeneration genuinely changes the voice, not just the color scheme. He selects all three lenses first to see the full picture, then narrows down to just Neuroscience + Symbolism (deliberately excluding Psychology) to see how those two specifically play off each other — a slicing move the app fully supports. The animated connecting lines redrawing as he changes selection is his favorite detail.

---

## Why two personas, one product

Maya and Theo represent the emotional-processing end and the curious-comparison end of the same value proposition. The journey structure, tone control, and lens-filtering are flexible enough to serve both without needing different modes — Maya narrows in deliberately and slowly; Theo explores broadly and combinatorially. Both are core, intended usage patterns.
